Reliability & Noise
Residual ICC on demons is 0.427, meaning roughly 57% of any single run's demon ordering is run-to-run noise. Re-running the same slate would reshuffle it substantially. The delta column (distance from base rate) carries more signal than the raw probability, but the entire ranking should be treated as exploratory measurement data, not stable predictions.
